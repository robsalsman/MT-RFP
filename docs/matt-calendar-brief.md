## What this is

A themed add-on pack for Matt, the 80s rock-star mascot: a playful
"charity calendar shoot" of classic 80s heartthrob tropes. Tone is
CAMPY and fun — winking at the genre (think mainstream 80s/90s
advertising and firefighter-calendar energy), never explicit and never
crude. Same character in every frame: same face, hair, red bandana (or
trope headgear where noted), same proportions as the model sheet.

Taste rules (hard): playful mainstream-calendar presentation only.
Shirtless or trope wardrobe as listed; nothing beyond that, no
suggestive emphasis, no innuendo props. Confident grin > smolder.

## Technical rules (same as all Matt art)

- Full body, feet near the bottom margin, front 3/4, **identical
  framing and scale to the attached pose**.
- Flat chroma green `#00b140` (or transparent), even lighting, no cast
  shadows, nothing cropped, canvas 1024×1536, zero text/watermarks.
